# Day 1: Uber Ride Matching Case Study

## Problem
At 8 PM in Noida Sector 62, many riders are requesting Uber rides, but requests fail because no drivers accept.

## Primary User
Riders

## Secondary User
Drivers

## User Impact
- Long waiting time
- Frustration
- Loss of trust
- Switch to competitors
- Missed important commitments

## Funnel Stage
Ride Confirmed → Driver Matched

## Possible Root Causes
- Low driver supply
- High peak-hour demand
- Heavy traffic
- Long pickup ETA
- Low driver fare attractiveness
- Bad pickup location
- Destination mismatch
- Payment preference issues

## Metrics to Check
- Match Success Rate
- Driver Acceptance Rate
- Request Timeout Rate
- Average Time to Match
- Average Pickup ETA
- Rider Cancellation Rate
- Driver Cancellation Rate
- Completed Trip Rate

## Product Solutions
- Increase driver incentives during peak hours
- Apply dynamic pricing
- Expand driver search radius carefully
- Suggest nearby pickup points
- Show alternate ride types
- Improve driver request sequencing
- Use demand prediction to pre-position drivers

## Business Impact
- Higher completed rides
- Lower cancellation
- Better rider retention
- Better driver utilization
- Higher revenue
- Stronger marketplace reliability
